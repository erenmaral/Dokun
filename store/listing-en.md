# Google Play store listing — English

**App name (max 30 characters):** Touch! Party Mini Games

**Short description (max 80 characters):**
Quick mini-game duels for 2-4 players on one phone or on nearby phones.

**Full description (max 4000 characters):**

Touch! is a party game made of quick mini games, each over in a few seconds, played on the same screen with your friends. Put the phone on the table, everyone take your zone, and start with 3-2-1!

🎮 20 MINI GAMES
• Reaction: The first to tap when it turns green wins. Don't fall for the fake yellow signal!
• Rapid Tap, Tile Run, Swipe Direction: Who has the fastest fingers?
• Dead Center: Stop the sliding ball as close to the green band as you can.
• Target Hunt, Rhythm, Whack-a-Mole: Be quick, accurate and on the beat.
• Number Order, Slider Puzzle, Odd One Out, Memory Match: Keep your eyes, mind and memory sharp.
• Color Trap, Quick Math, Arrow Direction, Count Them: A race of mental agility.
• Light Game, Color Sequence, Hold and Release, Find the Ball: Timing and attention.

👥 2, 3 OR 4 PLAYERS
The screen splits by number of players; text is flipped for the player across from you, so everyone reads comfortably from their own side. Multi-touch is supported.

🏆 MODES
• Classic: A set number of rounds, the highest score wins.
• Race: The first to reach the target score wins.
• Survival: Whoever doesn't win the round loses a life, the last one standing wins.
• Team: 4 players, 2 vs 2.
Each player picks their own color (6 colors: red, green, blue, yellow, purple, pink). With Mixed Match the games come in random order, or use Pick Games to choose your favorite game(s).

📡 YOUR OWN SCREEN ON EVERY PHONE
2-4 phones on the same Wi-Fi network (or a phone hotspot) connect with a short room code. No internet needed, no sign-up needed.

✨ MORE
• No ads, no accounts, no data collection
• Music and sound effects for every game, vibration feedback
• Pause, settings, player names
• Playable completely offline

Come on, who's faster?
